Payroll and Attendance Manual Testing Guide

Audience: HR and payroll testers

Purpose: Verify daily salary, salary-per-minute fines, working-day Basic Salary, overtime, public-holiday allowance, hourly pay, and final payslip totals.

Use a test database and fictional employees. Do not use real employee salary or personal information in screenshots or test evidence.

2. Daily Salary Calculation

Monthly Employee

Daily salary = Monthly wage ÷ 30

Example:
₹12,000 ÷ 30 = ₹400 per day

Daily Employee

Daily salary = Contract daily wage

Example:
Contract daily wage = ₹800
Daily salary = ₹800

Hourly Employee

Hourly pay = HR-approved payable hours × Contract hourly rate

Example:
7.5 hours × ₹100 = ₹750

Under the agreed workflow, hourly employees should not receive regular OT, public-holiday allowance, or late/fine deductions.

3. Salary-Per-Minute Fine

Scheduled minutes = Scheduled hours × 60
Salary per minute = Daily salary ÷ Scheduled minutes
Fine amount = Chargeable fine minutes × Salary per minute

Monthly Employee: Eight-Hour Schedule

Monthly wage       = ₹12,000
Daily salary       = ₹12,000 ÷ 30 = ₹400
Scheduled minutes  = 8 × 60 = 480
Salary per minute  = ₹400 ÷ 480 = ₹0.833333
10-minute fine     = 10 × ₹0.833333 = ₹8.33

Daily Employee: Eight-Hour Schedule

Daily wage         = ₹800
Scheduled minutes  = 480
Salary per minute  = ₹800 ÷ 480 = ₹1.666667
10-minute fine     = 10 × ₹1.666667 = ₹16.67

Monthly Employee: Split Shift

Morning            = 09:45–14:00 = 4h 15m
Afternoon          = 14:45–18:45 = 4h 00m
Scheduled minutes  = 495
Daily salary       = ₹12,000 ÷ 30 = ₹400
Salary per minute  = ₹400 ÷ 495 = ₹0.808081
70-minute fine     = 70 × ₹0.808081 = ₹56.57

7. Working-Day-Based Basic Salary

Basic pay = Monthly wage × Payable days ÷ Expected working days

Using currently available payslip fields:

expected_days = payslip.days_excl_weekend_holidays or 0.0
payable_days = payslip.total_working_days or 0.0

result = (
    (contract.wage or 0.0) * payable_days / expected_days
    if expected_days
    else 0.0
)

Example

Monthly wage          = ₹30,000
Expected working days = 25
Payable days          = 23.5
Per working day       = ₹30,000 ÷ 25 = ₹1,200
Basic pay             = ₹1,200 × 23.5 = ₹28,200
Important: total_working_days is attendance-based. Do not use this Basic formula in production until HR confirms how paid leave, unpaid leave, public holidays, weekly offs, joining dates, resignation dates, and approved regularization contribute to payable days.

9. Salary Structures

Monthly Salary Structure

  • Monthly Basic
  • Overtime
  • Public Holiday Allowance
  • Late/Fine deduction
  • Gross
  • Net

Daily Wage Structure

  • Daily Wage Basic
  • Overtime
  • Public Holiday Allowance
  • Late/Fine deduction
  • Gross
  • Net

Hourly Wage Structure

  • Hourly Pay
  • Gross
  • Net
  • Do not add OT, PH, Late/Fine, or Special Working Days

Every structure must have only one Basic, one Gross, and one Net rule. Do not include both LATE and LF. Do not include the legacy SWD rule.

10. Final Payslip Verification

Gross = Basic + Allowances
Net = Gross - Positive deductions

12. Release Acceptance

  • All OT, public-holiday, and fine entries have been reviewed.
  • Rejected entries contribute zero.
  • Public-holiday earnings are not duplicated in OT.
  • Hourly employees receive hourly pay only.
  • Date-wise Earnings and Deductions reconcile with salary-rule totals.
  • Gross and Net calculations reconcile manually.
  • Confirmed payslips retain their historical detail values.
